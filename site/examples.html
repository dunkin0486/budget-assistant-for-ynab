Example prompts

All three examples below use only read tools — nothing here creates, edits, or deletes anything in your YNAB plan.

For anyone new to YNAB

"How much do I have left to spend on groceries this month?"

Claude looks up your plan, finds the Groceries category for the current month, and tells you the remaining balance in plain language — e.g. "You've spent $312 of your $450 groceries budget this month — $138 left." If the category is overspent, it says so plainly.

For household plan managers

"Give me a plain-English summary of my plan status right now — what's covered, what's tight, and what still needs money assigned."

Claude pulls your current month's full category breakdown and groups it into fully funded, running low, and unfunded or overspent — plus your current Ready to Assign figure — so you get a briefing instead of a raw data dump.

For YNAB power users

"Why did I overspend on dining out the last three months? Show the trend."

Claude pulls Dining Out's budgeted/activity/balance for each of the last three months, then looks at the actual transactions behind any spike to explain what changed — not just numbers restated, but what drove them.
