Ask Claude about your YNAB budget

A Claude connector that answers questions about your YNAB budget in plain English — how much is left in a category, what your overall budget status looks like, and why spending moved the way it did.

See example prompts View on GitHub
Budget Assistant for YNAB is not made, endorsed, or officially supported by YNAB in any way. It's an independent, third-party connector built against YNAB's public API.

What it can do today

This is a read-only connector. It never creates, edits, or deletes anything in your YNAB budget — see the privacy policy for exactly what it reads and why.

Check a category balance

"How much do I have left to spend on groceries this month?"

Get a plain-English status

"Give me a summary of my budget right now — what's covered, what's tight, what still needs money."

Spot spending trends

"Why did I go over budget on dining out the last three months?"

Find what drove a spike

"What transactions pushed my entertainment category over this month?"

Who it's for

  • New to YNAB? Ask plain-language questions instead of hunting through categories yourself.
  • Managing a household budget? Get summaries you can actually hand to a partner.
  • A YNAB power user? Ask for trend analysis across months that YNAB's built-in reports don't give you directly.

How to connect

Add Budget Assistant for YNAB as a connector in Claude. You'll be asked to log in to YNAB and approve access — Claude never sees your YNAB password, and you can revoke access at any time from your YNAB account's connected-applications settings.
